As a part of our comprehensive effort to support people in Ukraine, Allset has initiated a fundraiser via UACC (Ukrainian American Coordinating Council) . Under this ongoing initiative, we print dedicated counter cards with QR codes for donations and distribute them across restaurants, coffee shops, and local food spots in major U.S. cities.
