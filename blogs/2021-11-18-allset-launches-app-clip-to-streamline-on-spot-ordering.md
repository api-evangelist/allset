This fall, we’re excited to launch the Allset App Clip for on-spot ordering and self-checkout at our partner locations nationwide, making it easier than ever to enjoy your favorite restaurants and coffee shops. What is the Allset App Clip? The Allset App Clip provides a specific app experience without downloading the full Allset app and a lengthy sign-up process.
