A message from Allset CEO, Stas Matviyenko. Dear Allset Team, On our All-Hands today, we shared that we made a difficult but prudent decision to adjust our strategy and reduce our workforce by 30% (29 people). Despite our efforts to avoid layoffs, our leadership team concluded that such restructuring is necessary for our company given the current economic downturn.
